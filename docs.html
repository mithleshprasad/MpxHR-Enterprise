What's New in Version 4.2

This guide covers everything in MpxHR up to version 4.2. The highlights of 4.2 come first, then the earlier 4.x highlights:

Onboarding

A joining checklist for every new joiner before an employee record exists, a printable joining form, and Make Employee in one click. (Section 22)

Joiner Form

Send a link; new joiners fill their details and upload documents from a phone. Choose any question, with checks and file limits, review the answers, then add them. (Section 22)

Reliable backups

Backups now always capture the database the program really uses, are checked after saving, keep the newest N, and restore with a safety copy. (Section 19)

New look for every list

Summary cards, avatars, chips and status pills on Employees, Attendance, Payroll, Payslips, Loans and the masters, plus a redesigned Salary Sheet with a totals row. (Section 4, 9)

Flexible salary structure

Redesigned payslips + Payslip Designer

New payslip layout with logo, loan details and amount in words, plus a drag-and-drop designer for your own layout. (Section 10)

Email system

Send payslips and letters by email, plus automatic leave-decision and birthday emails. Works with Gmail, Outlook, Zoho, Yahoo, any SMTP server, or a Google account. (Section 15)

Google Sync & Form Builder

Export tables to a Google Sheet, import rows through an approval queue, and build Google Forms that fill several tables at once. (Section 16)

Safer Excel imports

Preview screen with duplicate/date/status checks before anything is saved; correct DD/MM/YYYY dates; no more merging of different employees who share an email. (Section 14)

Bulk payroll edits

Select employees in Net Pay and set or add one salary head for all of them; import the amounts from Excel. (Section 9.3)

Custom Fields & Tables

Add your own fields to forms, or create new tables, with no code change. (Section 17)

Admin tools

DB Viewer, master recovery password, PAN field, application tour, keyboard-shortcut help, Data Security guide. (Sections 18, 20, 21)

1. Introduction & Overview

MpxHR Enterprise is a complete, offline-first Desktop HR Management System for small and mid-sized organisations. It runs on a local computer — no internet is needed for day-to-day use — and keeps all company, employee, attendance and payroll data on that machine. Internet is only used by the optional features: Email and Google Sync.

Multi-company. One installation can manage several companies and branches. You choose the active company at launch and can switch it any time from the top context bar.

4. Employees, Departments, Designations, Branches

4.1 Employees

Masters › Employees is the central record for every staff member. Click anywhere on a row to open the full detail NEW.

Adding an employee

  1. Click New.
  2. Fill personal details (name, DOB, gender, contact, address), employment details (department, designation, date of joining, status), salary structure and statutory numbers: PAN NEW, Aadhaar, PF, ESI, UAN.
  3. Fill any custom fields you have defined (Section 17).
  4. Add a photo and signature with the Browse buttons beside the form. After the first Save, the Documents box lets you upload Aadhaar, PAN, certificates and so on (Section 14.4).
  5. Click Save (Ctrl+S).
Salary structure. The salary structure is no longer limited to six fixed columns. It lists whichever Salary Heads you define in Masters › Salary Heads, each with its own amount per employee.

Editing, selecting, deleting

  • Tick one or more checkboxes (or the header checkbox to select all). Edit needs exactly one selected employee.
  • Delete works on many employees at once and removes their attendance, payroll, leave and document history. This cannot be undone — take a backup first.
  • Simultaneous Edit — inline bulk editing of many rows.
  • Import / Export / Template — Excel in and out (Section 14). Search plus Department/Branch filters narrow the grid.

4.2 Departments

Masters › Departments — simple list used for grouping and reporting everywhere.

4.3 Designations

Masters › Designations — job titles with a default base salary, LWF Category (Employee / Supervisor / Manager), Probation Period (days) and Deduct PT (whether Professional Tax applies).

4.4 Branches

Masters › Branches — office locations linked to a company; employees and attendance can be filtered by branch.

5. Salary Heads, Categories, Loans, Deductions

5.1 Salary Heads UPDATED

Masters › Salary Heads defines every pay component. The grid shows Description, Short Code, Type, Form 16 Purpose and flags for PF, ESI, Bonus, OT and Active.

  1. Click Add New, enter the description (for example House Rent Allowance) and short code.
  2. Choose Type: Earning or Deduction (required).
  3. Tick whether the head counts toward PF, ESI, Bonus and Overtime, choose its Form 16 purpose, and keep it Active.
  4. Save. The head is immediately available in each employee's salary structure, Net Pay, payslips and reports.

Use Modify to change a head and Delete to remove one. Existing payroll history keeps working, because older payroll data is read through the same dynamic structure.

5.2 Employee Categories

Masters › Categories — free-form groups (Staff, Worker, Management) for classification and bulk reporting.

5.3 Loan Setup

Masters › Loan Setup (also under the Payroll tab) — record a loan or advance once:

  1. Click New; pick the employee, loan type, principal, monthly EMI and start date.
  2. Save. The loan shows as Active with a running balance.

Each payroll run deducts the active EMI and reduces the balance. At zero the loan closes automatically; use Mark Closed to close it manually. Loan details also appear on the payslip.

5.4 User Defined Deductions

Masters › User Deductions — company-wide custom deductions (uniform fee, canteen). Each is a Fixed amount or a Percentage of Gross, and applies to every payroll run while Active.

9. Payroll Processing

9.1 Payroll months

Each Financial Year / Month has an Open or Closed status. Close a month once payroll is final to prevent accidental changes.

9.2 Generating payroll — Net Pay

Payroll › Net Pay shows every active employee's salary sheet, grouped into Employee Details, Income and Deductions. Click a row to open its full detail NEW.

  1. Confirm Company / Financial Year / Month in the context bar.
  2. Click the refresh icon to (re)calculate from attendance and the salary structure.
  3. Review or adjust cells if required.
  4. Click Save to commit the payroll for the period.

9.3 Bulk update of a salary head NEW

  1. Tick the employees in the Net Pay grid; the Bulk Update Head button appears.
  2. Choose the salary head and enter the amount.
  3. Select whether to Set the value or Add to the current value, then apply.
  4. Alternatively use Import Excel in the same window to load per-employee amounts from a spreadsheet.

9.4 Toolbar actions

  • Transfer TDS — mark the period's TDS as remitted, for records.
  • Challan — PDF summary of total PF, ESI, Professional Tax and TDS for statutory deposit.

Challan and Form 16 are computed from your payroll records for convenience. Always verify figures before filing.

10. Payslips & Payslip Designer

10.1 Payslips UPDATED

Payroll › Payslips lists every processed payslip for the period. The redesigned payslip shows the company logo (prominent) with a modest company name, income and deduction heads, loan details, amount in words and a footer disclaimer.

  • Click a row to preview; then Download PDF, Download Word or Print.
  • Tick several employees for the Bulk Export Wizard — many employees and months in one ZIP.
  • Email Selected NEW — tick employees and click it to email each their payslip as a PDF attachment (Section 15).

10.2 Payslip Designer NEW

Masters › Payslip Designer is an optional drag-and-drop editor for your own payslip layout. The standard layout is used until you save a design.

  1. Click a field in the left list to add it to the page, or add free text.
  2. Drag an element to move it; use the bottom-right handle to resize.
  3. In the property panel set exact X / Y / Width / Height (mm), font, size, weight, alignment and colour, and whether the field shows a label.
  4. Click Live Preview to see it with a real saved payroll record.
  5. Click Save. Use Reset to Default to return to the built-in layout, or Remove from payslip to delete the selected element.

Saved designs apply to the Payslips screen, Net Pay downloads and bulk ZIP exports alike — all use one shared payslip generator.

12. Documents & Letters

  1. Create or edit a template; insert placeholders using the tag buttons, for example {{name}}.
  2. Select the employee and the letter, then preview.
  3. Use Print, Download PDF / Word, or Email NEW to send it to the employee's registered address.

14. Importing Data & Files UPDATED

Employees and Attendance can be imported from Excel. Both now run a pre-import validation and show the findings in a preview window before anything is saved.

14.1 Steps

  1. Click Template (Employees) or Tpl (Attendance) to download the correct sheet layout, and fill it in.
  2. Click Import / Imp and choose the file.
  3. Read the preview: the row table plus a list of warnings and errors.
  4. If there are errors, tick the acknowledgement checkbox to unlock Import — or cancel, fix the sheet and try again. Warnings never block.

14.2 What is checked

ImportChecks
EmployeesDuplicate Employee Code within the file; the same email on rows with different names; missing First Name; unreadable DOB or date of joining
AttendanceUnknown employee codes; unreadable dates; duplicate employee + date pairs; unrecognised status values; missing Employee Code or Status

14.3 Good to know

  • Dates are read as DD/MM/YYYY (and Excel date serial numbers) — never silently as US-style MM/DD.
  • Employees sharing one email (a supervisor's, for example) are no longer merged: email only counts as a match when the first name also matches.
  • Salary columns such as BasicSalary and SpecialAllowance import into the salary structure that payroll reads.
  • Dropdown/Yes-No custom field values are trimmed and matched ignoring case.
  • Payroll itself has no Excel import — PF, ESI, TDS and Net Pay are computed from settings and attendance. Only salary-head amounts can be loaded in bulk (Section 9.3).

14.4 Photos, Signatures & Documents NEW

Employee photos, signatures and documents (Aadhaar, PAN, certificates ...) can be added one at a time in the employee form, or in bulk with Masters › Employees › Import Files. Bulk import works from a local folder, an Excel sheet, or a Google Drive folder, and always shows a preview before anything is saved.

One employee

  • Photo and Signature: use the Browse buttons beside the employee form. The picture is checked against the current limits and scaled down if very large.
  • Documents: open an existing employee, type a document type (or pick one from the list), click Upload File. Use the eye icon to open a document and the bin icon to delete it.

Many employees — Import Files

  1. Click Import Files in the Employees toolbar and choose the source below.
  2. Click Preview. Every file is listed with the employee it matches, where it will go (Photo, Signature or Document: type), its size, and a note if it will be skipped.
  3. Click Import. Skipped files are left out; the rest are saved, and a final summary lists anything that failed.
SourceHow files are matched to employees
Local folderBy file name, starting with the employee code: EMP001.jpg or EMP001_photo.jpg = photo; EMP001_signature.png = signature; EMP001_Aadhaar.pdf = document of type “Aadhaar”. A sub-folder per employee also works: EMP001/photo.jpg, EMP001/PAN.pdf.
Excel sheetOne row per employee. Columns: Employee Code, then Photo, Signature and one column per document type. Each cell is a file name (looked up in the folder you choose), a full path, or a Google Drive link. A template can be downloaded in the wizard. Excel cannot hold the files themselves, only their names or links.
Google Drive folderPut the files in one Drive folder using the same naming as a local folder. Choose the Google connection and give the folder link, id or name. MpxHR reads and downloads the files through your Google script.
Google script update needed. Drive import needs the latest script. In Settings › Google Sync › Connect a Sheet, copy the script, paste it over the old one in Apps Script, deploy again, and approve the Drive permission if Google asks. The folder must be accessible to the Google account that owns the script. A Google Form cannot be created with a file-upload question by the script; if you add one by hand in the Form editor, Google stores the uploads in Drive and the response sheet contains links, which the Excel source can use.

Limits — set by you

Open Masters › Settings › Files & Uploads to choose, separately for Photo, Signature and Documents, the maximum size (0.05 to 50 MB) and the allowed file types (comma-separated, no dots, for example pdf, docx, jpg). The limits apply to every route — employee form, folder, Excel and Drive — and files outside them are listed as skipped with the reason. Photo and signature accept picture types only; program and script files (exe, bat, js, html and similar) are always refused.

Signature and photo can be placed in letters with the Signature (image) and Photo (image) tag buttons in the letter editor (Section 12).

15. Email Setup & Automatic Emails NEW

MpxHR can email payslips and letters and send automatic notifications. It is configured once for the whole app at Masters › Settings › Email, where a built-in Setup Guide also explains each step.

15.1 Choose a provider

ProviderWhen to use
SMTP serverRecommended. Gmail, Outlook / Microsoft 365, Zoho, Yahoo or your company mail server. One-click presets fill host, port and security.
Google accountWhen SMTP or App Passwords are not possible. Uses the Google Sync script; no password is stored in MpxHR.

15.2 Gmail via SMTP

  1. In your Google Account › Security, turn on 2-Step Verification.
  2. Search App passwords, create one named “MpxHR” and copy the 16-letter code.
  3. In MpxHR click the Gmail preset. Username = full Gmail address; Password = the App Password (not your normal password).
  4. Fill From Name and From Email (use the same address), click Save Email Settings.
  5. Type your own address beside Send Test and click it. If it arrives, everything else will work.

Outlook: use the Outlook / 365 preset (smtp.office365.com, port 587, STARTTLS); your IT admin may need to enable Authenticated SMTP for the mailbox. Other hosts: port 587 with STARTTLS, or 465 with SSL/TLS.

15.3 What can be emailed

Birthday wishesAutomatic, once a day when MpxHR is opened, if ticked
Each employee needs a valid Email in their profile; others are skipped and listed as failed. Gmail limits a normal account to about 500 emails a day.

Edit subject and body for Payslip, Letter, Leave Approved, Leave Rejected and Birthday under Email Templates, then Save Templates. Placeholders are replaced when sending:

16. Google Sync & Form Builder NEW

Masters › Settings › Google Sync connects MpxHR to a Google Sheet through a small script that runs under your own Google account. There is no Google sign-in inside MpxHR, no Cloud project and nothing to install; the shared secret is stored encrypted on your PC.

16.1 Connect a Sheet

  1. Click Connect a Sheet and follow the on-screen instructions to copy the script into Apps Script from your Sheet and deploy it.
  2. Paste the resulting web-app link and secret back into MpxHR.
If you connected before email was added, recopy the latest script over the old one and redeploy so email sending works.
Whenever MpxHR needs a newer script (for the Joiner Form or Google Forms) it opens a short guide: copy the script, paste it in Apps Script, Deploy › Manage deployments › pencil › New version, allow the permission, then press Check now. Never choose "New deployment": that changes your link.

16.2 Table Sync

Click New Sync to link a database table with a Sheet tab. Tables available: Employees, Departments, Designations, Attendance, Loans, Assets (and your custom tables). Syncs can be edited in place.

  • Export — write a table to a tab.
  • Import — rows pulled from the tab go to the Review Queue. Column mapping is guessed automatically and you can adjust it.
  • A preview shows what a sync will do before it runs.

16.3 Review Queue

Nothing becomes a real record until you approve it. Each mapping gets its own table; cells with problems are highlighted amber with a warning icon (hover for the reason).

Checked on importBehaviour
Dates (DOB, joining)Read as DD/MM/YYYY and stored in a standard format; invalid values are flagged
Duplicate keys in the same sheetFlagged on the row (for example, one email on two employees) instead of being silently skipped
Employee last nameOptional

16.4 Form Builder

Design a Google Form from any table's fields — or link several tables (for example Employee + Loan) so one submission fills them all. Generate the form through the connected Sheet, share the link, and review incoming responses in a section-by-section preview before approving them into real records.

19. Backup, Restore & Data Security

Masters › Settings › Database Maintenance shows the date and contents of your last backup at the top. A card turns green when the last backup is recent and amber when it is a week old or more, or when none has been taken.

19.1 Back up now

  • Everything (data + documents) — a .zip with the database and every uploaded file (documents, joiner files). This is the safest choice.
  • Data only — a compact .sqlite file with all records. Type a password first to save it encrypted (.enc). Remember the password: without it an encrypted backup cannot be opened.
  • MpxHR takes a consistent copy of the database even while you are working, checks the file after saving, and tells you what it holds (for example "44 employees, 1 company, 123 payroll rows").
  • Save a copy on a pen drive or in a Google Drive / OneDrive folder, not only on this computer.

19.2 Automatic backup

  • Tick Back up automatically every time I close MpxHR, and optionally Also include uploaded documents.
  • Save in folder — choose a folder (a Drive/OneDrive-synced one keeps a copy in the cloud). Open shows it in Windows. If the folder is not available (for example a pen drive that is unplugged) the backup is saved in a safe default folder instead of being lost.
  • Keep the latest — how many automatic backups to keep (1 to 200). Older ones are deleted.
  • Back up now to this folder makes an automatic-style backup immediately.

19.3 Recent automatic backups

The list shows the newest automatic backups with date and size. Click Restore on one to bring it back without browsing for a file.

19.4 Restore from a file

Click Choose a backup file (.zip, .sqlite or .enc) and type the password for an encrypted one. Restoring replaces all current data and documents.

  • MpxHR first checks the file: the password, that it is a MpxHR database, and its integrity. A wrong password or a damaged file stops the restore and changes nothing.
  • A safety copy of the data you had is saved in the backups › before-restore folder (the last five are kept), so a mistaken restore can be undone.
  • Files from a zip are written only inside MpxHR's uploads folder. Afterwards MpxHR restarts so every screen loads the restored data.

19.5 Where is my data?

In the installed program the database is %APPDATA%\mpxhr-desktop\database.sqlite and the uploaded files are in the uploads folder beside it. Backups made with MpxHR 4.1.x or older did not reliably include the database, so after upgrading to 4.2 take a new Everything backup straight away.

Take a fresh backup before restoring, before upgrading MpxHR and before any large import.

19.6 Built-in protection

  • Passwords are stored hashed; the Google Sync secret is encrypted with the operating system's secure storage.
  • Administrative actions (deleting records, restore, DB Viewer, raw queries) need an active administrator session.
  • Only one copy of MpxHR can run at a time, protecting the database from write conflicts.
  • Company data is isolated per company; activity is written to a local diagnostic log for support.
  • Masters › Data Security opens a short in-app guide to these practices.

22. Onboarding & the Joiner Form NEW

Masters › Onboarding helps you receive a new person properly before an employee record exists: collect their details and documents, follow a joining checklist, and turn them into an employee with one click.

22.1 Add a new joiner

  1. Click New Joiner and enter the name, email, phone, department, designation, branch and expected joining date.
  2. Save. The standard joining checklist is created for that person (documents to collect, HR steps, IT and admin steps).
  3. The list shows each joiner with a progress bar and a status: Pending, In progress or Completed. The cards at the top count them.

22.2 The checklist

  • Select a joiner and click Checklist (or double-click the row). Tick a step when it is done, or add and remove your own steps. The status changes by itself.
  • Checklist Template edits the standard list. Write one step per line as Group | Task. It applies to new joiners; people already added keep their list. Restore default brings back the original steps.
  • Print Form prints a joining form with blank lines for the joiner to fill by hand, plus the checklist, with signature lines.

22.3 Make Employee

Select the joiner and click Make Employee. MpxHR creates the employee with the name, email, phone, company, branch, department, designation and joining date, ticks the "Employee record created" step, and copies everything the joiner sent through the form (see below): profile fields, custom fields, photo, signature and documents. Then open Masters › Employees to add the employee code, salary and the remaining details. The employee limit of your license applies as usual.

22.4 The Joiner Form (online link)

Let the joiner fill in their own details and upload documents from a phone. It runs on your own Google Sheet script (the same one used by Google Sync, Section 16). MpxHR never stores your Google login.

  1. Choose the questions. Onboarding › Joiner Form › Questions & documents. Tick what the joiner should upload (photo, Aadhaar copy, PAN copy, bank proof, certificates, relieving letter ...) and fill in (PAN number, UAN, date of birth, address, bank details ...). Name, mobile, email, department, designation, joining date and notes are always asked. Tick Must fill for compulsory ones. You can also add your own question.
  2. Get the link. Click Get link / Share. The first time (and after an MpxHR update) a short five-step guide appears to paste the new script into your Google Sheet; the Check now button tells you which step is still missing. Then copy the link or send it by WhatsApp or email.
  3. Joiner fills the form. The answer is saved as a new row in the tab New Joiners of your Sheet; uploaded files go to a private folder MpxHR Joiner Uploads in your Google Drive.
  4. See what came in. View responses lists every submission (read-only): new ones and already-added ones, with answers and document names. Nothing is added to Onboarding yet.
  5. Add them. Add new joiners downloads the documents to this computer, creates each joiner with the checklist, and ticks the matching checklist steps (for example "Aadhaar card copy"). People already in the list (same email or phone) are skipped.

22.5 Where each answer goes, checks and limits

Open Options on a question to change the defaults, which are already sensible:

SettingWhat it does
Save the answer toKeep with the joiner only; an Employee profile field (PAN, Aadhaar, bank, address ...) or an Employee custom field; for files: the employee photo, signature or a document type. Applied when you click Make Employee.
Check the answer isPAN (ABCDE1234F), Aadhaar (12 digits), IFSC, UAN (12 digits), PIN code (6 digits), mobile (10 digits), bank account (6–20 digits), email, letters only, digits only. Spaces are tidied and PAN/IFSC are made capital automatically.
Longest answer, smallest / largestLimits for text length and number ranges. Dropdown answers must be one of the choices.
Biggest file, allowed typesPer file question, up to 5 MB (a Google script limit) and PDF, JPG, PNG or DOCX. A photo or signature can only be JPG or PNG. The limits in Settings › Files & Uploads also apply when the file is brought in.
Tick checklist stepWhich checklist step becomes done when this answer or file arrives.

The checks run three times — on the form page, in your script, and again in MpxHR — so a hand-made request cannot slip a bad value through.

The link is public, the data is not. Anyone with the link can submit the form, but nobody can read the answers through it; they exist only in your Sheet and Drive. The same phone number cannot submit twice within a minute, and a hidden field blocks simple bots.

22.6 Google Form (advanced)

In Onboarding › Joiner Form you can also build a real Google Form (Section 16). It asks the fixed joiner fields and its answers wait in the review queue. It needs the extra Google Forms permission in your script and cannot take file uploads, so the online link above is recommended. Forms have Preview (shows the questions as they will be asked) and Share (copy the link, send it by WhatsApp or email).
