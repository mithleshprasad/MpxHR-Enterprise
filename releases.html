Release notes

What changed in each version. Current version: 4.2.0.

Version 4.2.0

2026-10-04
  • New Onboarding module: joining checklist for every new joiner, printable joining form and one-click Make Employee.
  • Joiner Form: send a link, new joiners fill details and upload documents from a phone. Pick any questions, with checks and file-size limits, then review and add the answers.
  • Reliable backups: always the real database, verified after saving, encrypted option, keep the newest N, and restore with a safety copy. Fixes backups that missed the database in installed copies of 4.1.x.
  • New look for every list: summary cards, avatars and status pills on Employees, Attendance, Payroll, Payslips, Loans and the masters; redesigned Salary Sheet with a totals row.
  • Settings menu scrolls, Logout is never cut off, and Google Forms have Preview, Share and a guided setup.

Version 4.1.2

2026-10-02
  • A license chip is always visible in the top bar, so the plan and days left are never hidden.
  • License moved near the top of the Settings list.

Version 4.1.1

2026-10-02
  • One Signature box on the employee form (older custom signature fields are merged automatically).

Version 4.1.0

2026-10-02
  • License keys with a 14-day trial (up to 50 employees), yearly plans with a 15-day grace period and a one-time Lifetime plan.
  • View-only mode when a license ends: view, print and export everything, no data is ever locked.

Version 4.0.1

2026-10-02
  • The MpxHR icon and version information are embedded in the installed app.

Version 4.0.0

2026-10-02
  • Modern look across the app: top bar, Dashboard, Analytics with 3D charts, Reports Center, Letters, tables with status pills and new add/edit popups.
  • Photo, signature and document intake with bulk Import Files from a folder, Excel or Google Drive and admin-set limits.
  • Safer sign-in: show/hide password, Caps Lock warning, remembered user name and temporary lock after wrong attempts.
  • Fixed login inputs not clickable after logout.

Version 3.3.0

2026-10-02
  • Rewritten user guide covering email, Google Sync, Payslip Designer and custom fields.

Version 3.2.0

2026-10-02
  • Email system (SMTP or Google) with templates for payslips, letters, leave decisions and birthdays.
  • Google Sync review queue; last name optional when syncing employees.
  • Deduction heads on the payslip, Head Ded. column and Excel import for bulk head updates.

Version 3.1.x

2026-09-30
  • Pre-import validation for Employee and Attendance Excel imports.
  • Correct DD/MM/YYYY date reading; employees sharing an email are no longer merged.
  • Financial-year dropdown generated dynamically; payslip layout fixes; attendance history and bulk payroll edit.

Version 3.0.0

2026-09-27
  • Dynamic salary heads, redesigned payslips, DB Viewer, PAN field and master password recovery.

Version 2.3.0

2026-09-19
  • Payroll correctness fixes, better company data isolation and scale performance; Google Sync (Apps Script gateway).

Version 2.2.0

2026-05-25
  • First release: employees, attendance, leave, payroll, payslips and reports.
