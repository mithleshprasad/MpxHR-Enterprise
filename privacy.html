Privacy policy

Short and plain.

This page is a plain-language template. Please have it reviewed by a legal adviser and update the company details before relying on it.

The software

MpxHR Enterprise runs on your computer. Company, employee, attendance and payroll data is stored in a database file on that computer. We do not receive, upload or have access to this data.

Optional features

If you switch on Email, MpxHR connects to the mail server you configure to send the messages you choose. If you switch on Google Sync, it connects to the Google Sheet, Form or Drive folder you link. These connections go from your computer directly to those services, under their own terms.

License

A license key is checked on your computer. No machine identifier or personal data is sent to us.

This website

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. If you contact us through WhatsApp, phone or email we receive the details you choose to share and use them only to reply to you.

Your responsibility

As the owner of your HR data you are responsible for backups, access control and compliance with the laws that apply to you.
